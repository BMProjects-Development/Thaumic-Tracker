# Thaumic Tracker 1.0

The first release adds a compact research tracker for Thaumaturge.

- Pin one research entry using its ribbon in the Thaumonomicon.
- Track items, crafting, prerequisite research, theory notes, and observation
  aspects on your HUD.
- Follow research stages and completion automatically.
- Hide or show the panel and page through long lists with configurable keys.
- Restore your selection for each world, server, and player account.
- Track custom research from datapacks and KubeThaum.
- English and Russian translations and guides.
